What UV Filters Are in Beauty of Joseon Relief Sun?

The formula uses four UV filters: Diethylamino Hydroxybenzoyl Hexyl Benzoate, Ethylhexyl Triazone, Methylene Bis-Benzotriazolyl Tetramethylbutylphenol, and Diethylhexyl Butamido Triazone.

Official INCI name Main role in the filter system
Diethylamino Hydroxybenzoyl Hexyl Benzoate A photostable organic filter used primarily for UVA protection, especially long-wave UVA.
Ethylhexyl Triazone A highly efficient and photostable UVB filter that helps support the product's high SPF claim.
Methylene Bis-Benzotriazolyl Tetramethylbutylphenol (Nano) Also called MBBT or bisoctrizole. A particulate organic filter that contributes broad UVA and UVB coverage.
Diethylhexyl Butamido Triazone A photostable organic filter that mainly strengthens UVB and shorter-wave UVA protection.

This is not a zinc oxide or titanium dioxide mineral sunscreen. It is commonly described as a chemical sunscreen because its protection comes from organic UV filters. MBBT is technically a particulate organic filter, which makes the simple chemical-versus-mineral label less precise than the actual ingredient breakdown.

What the filter system means

The filter combination is one of the strongest parts of this formula. It combines dedicated UVA and UVB filters rather than relying on one or two older filters to do most of the work.

Is Beauty of Joseon Relief Sun Fragrance-Free?

Yes. Beauty of Joseon currently labels Relief Sun fragrance-free, and the official ingredient list does not include parfum, fragrance, or conventional fragrant essential oils.

Fragrance-free does not mean that no user can react to the formula. Sunscreen filters, preservatives, surfactants, botanical extracts, or other ingredients can still cause irritation in a small number of people. However, removing added fragrance makes the formula more sensible for many users with fragrance sensitivity or acne-prone skin.

Does Beauty of Joseon Relief Sun Contain Alcohol?

The current official ingredient list contains t-Butyl Alcohol and Behenyl Alcohol, while the same product page labels the sunscreen alcohol free. The page does not explain exactly how the brand defines that claim.

These two ingredients are not interchangeable. Behenyl Alcohol is a fatty alcohol used to support texture, consistency, and emollience. Fatty alcohols generally behave differently from ethanol or denatured alcohol and are not automatically drying.

t-Butyl Alcohol is a volatile alcohol and appears much lower in the ingredient list. Its presence does not automatically make the sunscreen harsh, but it does mean that the broad phrase "contains no alcohol at all" would be inaccurate when describing the current official INCI.

For strict alcohol avoidance

If you avoid every ingredient containing the word alcohol, this formula does not meet that standard. Check the packaging of your exact unit because regional or future formulations may differ.

Does Beauty of Joseon Relief Sun Contain Niacinamide?

Yes. Niacinamide is the eighth ingredient in the current official list. Beauty of Joseon does not state its exact percentage on the product page.

Niacinamide is one of the more useful supporting ingredients in the formula. Research supports its role in skin-barrier function, uneven tone, sebum regulation, and visible signs of photoaging. In this sunscreen it should be viewed as a supportive skincare ingredient, not as a substitute for the UV filters or a dedicated pigmentation treatment.

Its relatively high position indicates that it is not merely the final trace botanical in the formula, but ingredient order alone cannot reveal the exact concentration. Avoid websites that assign a precise niacinamide percentage without a direct brand disclosure or formula documentation.

Rice, Ferments and Other Supporting Ingredients.

Outside the filter system, Relief Sun is structured more like a lightweight moisturizing cream than a dry-touch sports sunscreen. That helps explain why many people find it easy to apply in a full sunscreen amount.

Rice extract and rice germ extract Brand hero

Beauty of Joseon states that the formula contains 30% rice extract. Rice-derived cosmetic ingredients have supportive evidence for moisturizing, antioxidant, soothing, and brightening effects, but the sunscreen filters remain the main protective ingredients.

Niacinamide Barrier support

A well-studied supporting ingredient that can contribute to barrier function, tone, oil regulation, and signs of photoaging.

Propanediol, glycerin, butylene glycol and pentylene glycol Humectants

These ingredients help hold water in the upper layers of the skin and support the formula's comfortable, moisturizer-like character.

Dibutyl adipate, coco-caprylate/caprate and caprylyl methicone Texture

These emollient and sensorial ingredients improve spread, dissolve or disperse filters, and reduce the chalky feel associated with some sunscreens.

Tocopherol, green tea, ginseng and kelp extracts Antioxidant support

Useful supporting ingredients with antioxidant or soothing rationale, but they are not the principal reason the sunscreen provides protection.

Fermented grain and plant extracts Formula extras

These support the product's skincare story. Evidence for fermented skincare ingredients varies greatly by organism, substrate, concentration, and final formula, so they should not be treated as a guaranteed probiotic treatment.

Is It Good for Sensitive or Acne-Prone Skin?

The fragrance-free design and moisturizer-like base make Relief Sun a reasonable option for many people with sensitive or acne-prone skin. Beauty of Joseon also reports small product studies supporting non-sensitizing and acne-prone-skin suitability. Those brand-reported results are useful context, but they do not guarantee that every sensitive or acne-prone user will tolerate the formula.

Decyl Glucoside Patch test

A mild surfactant and dispersing ingredient that is tolerated by most users but can be relevant in contact allergy for a small group of people.

Ethylhexylglycerin and multiple botanical extracts Individual sensitivity

Usually well tolerated, but highly reactive skin can respond to almost any complex formula. Patch testing is sensible if you have a history of sunscreen or cosmetic allergy.

The product is not automatically non-comedogenic for every person simply because the brand uses that label. Acne triggers are individual, and breakouts can also result from under-cleansing, heavy layering, sweat, or another product in the routine.

How to Use Beauty of Joseon Relief Sun Properly

Apply it as the final step of your morning skincare routine. Use a generous, even layer over all exposed areas. Do not rely on a tiny cosmetic amount simply because the formula spreads easily.

For normal or dry skin

Use moisturizer underneath when your skin needs it. The sunscreen is moisturizing, but it does not have to replace your moisturizer every day. Use our sunscreen-versus-moisturizer decision guide to judge the one-step option by skin type, climate and comfort.

For oily skin

Try it without a separate moisturizer first. Adding several hydrating layers underneath can make the finish heavier than necessary.

Under makeup

Allow the sunscreen to settle before applying complexion products. Pilling depends on the complete routine, not only on the sunscreen.

Outdoor exposure

Reapply regularly during meaningful sun exposure. Choose a clearly labeled water-resistant sunscreen for swimming, sports, heavy sweating, or long beach days.

Final Verdict: Is the Formula Good?

Yes. On paper, Beauty of Joseon Relief Sun is a well-constructed daily sunscreen. Its strongest features are the four-filter UVA and UVB system, high niacinamide placement, comfortable humectant and emollient base, and lack of listed fragrance.

The rice and ferment ingredients add a credible skincare story, but they should not distract from the filter system. The main weakness is not poor formulation. It is that the product is not positioned as water resistant, can be too dewy for some oily skin types, and carries an alcohol-free label that deserves clearer explanation because the official INCI includes t-Butyl Alcohol and Behenyl Alcohol.

8.5 out of 10

A strong everyday sunscreen formula for normal, dry, dehydrated, and many combination skin types. The rating reflects the ingredient list, filter design, available evidence, and product positioning. It is not a controlled wear-test score.
